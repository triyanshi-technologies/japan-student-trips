Food & dietary needs

We collect vegetarian, Jain, egg-free and allergy needs early and confirm each one with hotels and restaurants in writing. Indian meals can be arranged on some evenings if you'd like.

Visa & documentation

Checklists, invitation and itinerary letters, and a timeline that works backwards from departure. Decisions are made by the Embassy of Japan — we never promise approval.

Stays & movement

Clean, central hotels with twin or triple rooms, separate floors where possible, private coaches and pre-booked rail seats so the group stays together.
